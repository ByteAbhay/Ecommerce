E-commerce Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses 2 years of e-commerce orders (Oct 2023 – Oct 2025) across regions, cities, product categories and payment methods.

📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to turn raw order data into clear business answers:

How much are we selling, and how profitable is it?
Which regions, cities and categories perform best?
Which payment methods do customers prefer?
Who are the top customers?
📂 Dataset
Detail	Info
Rows	5,000 orders
Columns	14
Period	04 Oct 2023 – 03 Oct 2025
Regions	North, South, East, West
Cities	20 Indian cities
Categories	10 (Home Decor, Furniture, Clothing, Books, Kitchen, Electronics, Toys, Sports, Beauty, Groceries)
Payment modes	UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, COD

Columns: Order ID, Order Date, Customer Name, Region, City, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Sales, Profit, Payment Mode.

🔍 Key Insights
North is the top region by sales (₹14.36 Cr), followed by East, West and South.
Home Decor has the highest sales (₹5.72 Cr); Groceries has the lowest.
Furniture earns the highest profit; Groceries earns the lowest.
Bangalore is the top city by sales, while Thiruvananthapuram is the lowest.
2024 was the strongest year in sales.
Sales are spread fairly evenly across categories and regions, so no single segment dominates.
